How email alerts work

Use the Countries button above to pick which countries and metrics you want to follow, and turn on "Email me when something I track is coming up" in that same panel. That's the whole setup, there's nothing else to configure.

Once it's on, a daily check runs against everything you're tracking. If anything is due within the next 7 days, you get one email that morning listing what's coming, the country, the release, and the date, so you don't have to keep this page open to stay on top of it.

  • One email a day at most, never more, and never if nothing you track is due that week
  • Only covers what you've explicitly ticked, add a country or a metric and it's included from the next check onward, remove one and it stops
  • You can turn it off any time from the same Countries panel

This is a new feature and still being tested behind the scenes, admin preview only for now, not yet available more widely.
