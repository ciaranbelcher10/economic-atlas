    On the roadmap: federal debt stock, and a breakdown of where public money goes.

    Data: FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (IMF, OECD), the OECD and the World Bank. Series codes are shown with each metric; figures are the latest published observations and are subject to revision. Exports and imports individually (rather than as a combined trade balance) are a known gap for Brazil, not yet wired to a verified live source; see the note on the Trade tab. A 10-year government bond yield is also a known gap: no live OECD/FRED mirror could be found for Brazil specifically, unlike most other countries on this site. Unemployment is annual (World Bank), not monthly, since the usual monthly mirror for Brazil stopped updating in 2015. Nominal GDP here uses a not-seasonally-adjusted series (the seasonally-adjusted version stopped updating in 2025), so quarter-on-quarter GDP growth may look noisier than other countries' pages.
