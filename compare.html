Click any column to sort. Shows whatever time horizon is selected above.

Figures: ONS, FRED, Eurostat, e-Stat/Bank of Japan, MoSPI, Bank of Korea and OECD — the same sources already cited on each country's own page. Historical comparison points are the closest available observation to each horizon, not necessarily an exact anniversary. GDP growth over a horizon (1Y/2Y/5Y/10Y) shows total real growth over that period, compounded from the country's own real GDP level — not the difference between two single quarterly growth-rate readings, which wouldn't tell you how much the economy actually grew. India's unemployment series has limited history (MoSPI data begins in 2025), so its 5Y/10Y comparison points aren't meaningful and are marked accordingly. The Eurozone is excluded from the map view since it isn't a single country UN Comtrade or world-atlas can plot — it still appears in every chart and table above.
