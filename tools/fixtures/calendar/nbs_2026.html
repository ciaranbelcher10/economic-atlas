Notes:

1. The release dates are preliminary and subject to adjustment.

2. The content scheduled for release in this table (excluding Purchasing Managers’ Index) presents the indicator data of the previous month, previous quarter, previous year, or cumulative totals.

3. Annual and quarterly national economic performance will be released in January, April, July, and October, respectively. Monthly national economic performance will be released in March, May, June, August, September, November, and December. Alongside the press release on national economic performance, separate press releases will also be issued on the monthly or cumulative data of industrial production, energy production, fixed asset investment, investment in real estate development and sales, and total retail sales of consumer goods.

4. According to the statistical report system, the data of industrial production operation, energy production, fixed asset investment, real estate development investment and sales, total retail sales of consumer goods, and industrial economic benefits above the designated size for January will not be released in February.

5. Due to the impact of the Spring Festival, the monthly report on Purchasing Managers’ Index for February will be released on March 4.

6. In principle, the ten-day reporting data on market prices of important means of production in circulation will be released at 9:30 on the 4th, 14th, and 24th of each month; the ten-day report on market prices of important means of production in circulation in mid-February and early October will be cancelled due to the Spring Festival and National Day holidays. The ten-day report for late April will be released on May 7, and that for late September will be released on October 9.

7. The Survey and Monitoring Report on Migrant Workers for 2025 is expected to be released in April.

8. The Average Annual Wages of Persons Employed in Urban Units for 2025 is expected to be released in May.

9. The Development Report on Culture and Related Industries for 2025 is expected to be released in June.

10. The New Economic Growth Drivers Index for 2025 is expected to be released in August.

11. The summer grain output, early rice output, and total grain output for the year are expected to be released in July, August, and December, respectively.

12. The Communiqué on National Expenditures on Science and Technology for 2025 and China’s Innovation Index for 2025 are expected to be released between September and October.

13. The value added of the “Three New” Economy for 2025 is expected to be released in July. The data of the value added of national culture and related industries, tourism and related industries, the sports industry, patent-intensive industries, and agriculture and related industries for 2025 are expected to be released following the final verification of GDP for 2025.

14. National statistical database (http://data.stats.gov.cn/) updates all progress-tracking indicator data in approximately three working days following the data release. The annual data will be updated three times. The primary data will be updated in approximately two weeks after the release of theStatistical Communiqué on the 2025 National Economic and Social Developmentand the publication of theChina Statistical Abstract 2026, while the full annual data will be updated in approximately two months after the publication of theChina Statistical Yearbook 2026.

15. The official Weibo account of the National Bureau of Statistics of China, @中国统计(China Statistics), and the WeChat official account, “统计微讯(Statistics Micro News)” will post relevant information on press releases following data releases. You are welcome to follow the accounts for more updates. Please visit the following link to scan the QR code: http://www.stats.gov.cn/hd/xmt/202302/t20230217_1912856.html.
