What this is

The Economic Atlas publishes official economic statistics, refreshed automatically from national statistics offices, central banks and international bodies (see each page's own footer for exact sources). By using the site -- browsing, creating an account, subscribing to Pro, or buying credits -- you're agreeing to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the site.

Accounts

You need an account to download data or use Customise & export. You're responsible for keeping your login details to yourself and for anything that happens under your account. You must be old enough to enter into a binding contract in your own country to create one. One account per person -- please don't share logins to get around the credits system.

Free trial

New accounts get 14 days of Pro automatically, no card required. It ends on its own after 14 days and your account reverts to the free plan unless you've subscribed to Pro before then -- nothing is ever auto-charged at the end of a trial, since no payment details were taken to start one.

Pro subscription and credits

Pro is a recurring monthly subscription (currently £5.99/mo, billed via Stripe) giving unlimited data downloads and Customise & export. If you'd rather not subscribe, credits let you pay per use instead -- 50p per data download, £1 per Customise & export, purchased in top-ups of £2 or more. Credits don't expire and carry no cash value outside the site; they're not a currency, gift card, or refundable deposit, though we'll always sort out a genuine payment error if one occurs -- see Contact below.

We'll try to make any material change to what an existing subscription includes reasonably clear, but a feature being available in Pro today isn't a permanent guarantee it stays there unchanged forever.

Billing and cancellation

Stripe handles all payment processing; we never see or store your card details ourselves. You can cancel Pro any time from your account's billing portal -- cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and we don't provide refunds for partial months.
