Roadmap

  1. Monetary policy dashboard — a page for every Bank of England rate decision, facts only: previous and new rate, the vote split, an AI summary of the Monetary Policy Summary, minutes and press conference (summarising what was said, never opinion), a "what changed" comparison against the previous statement, market-implied rate expectations before and after (SONIA/OIS), market reaction in gilts, sterling and the FTSE, the economic context, a browsable archive of every MPC meeting, and links to every primary source.
  2. Trade partner map — visualise the UK's largest trading partners on an interactive world map.
  3. Country comparison tool — compare any two countries side by side (e.g. UK vs Germany, Japan vs South Korea) across GDP, inflation, unemployment and more.
  4. Economic Health Score — a single composite score per country drawn from GDP growth, inflation, unemployment, debt, current account and productivity, for at-a-glance comparison.
  5. Timeline slider — drag through years on the front-page map and watch the figures update.
  6. Labour market breakdowns — unemployment and inactivity by age group and sector.
  7. Public finances deep dive — total debt stock, debt interest as a share of spending (Debt Management Office data), and where public money goes.
  8. Interactive world map front page — click any live country to open its page.
  9. Next countries — South Korea and Brazil next, prioritising consistency and depth over sheer number of countries covered.
  10. China page — pending a reliable high-frequency data source; free Western mirrors of Chinese statistics are sparse, and stale data would break the freshness promise.
  11. Markets — FX, equity indices and gilt yields alongside the macro data.

Have an idea or want an indicator added? Suggest it — the roadmap is shaped by what readers ask for.
