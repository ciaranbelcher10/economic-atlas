About Economic Atlas

Economic Atlas is a straightforward, self-updating reference for how the world's major economies are performing. The figures rebuild themselves automatically from the official statistics that governments, central banks and international bodies publish directly. Nothing is scraped from third parties, and nothing is modelled or estimated by us.

The site follows one rule above everything else: show the figures, not an opinion about them. Every number here traces back to an official source, and the site does not interpret, forecast, or take a view on what any of it means. That's a deliberate choice, not an oversight. The aim is a resource that reads the same way regardless of who's looking at it or what they already believe, with no political lean or editorial slant in how the data is presented.

Compare, Dashboard and the country pages simply offer different ways of looking at that same underlying data, never a different version of it. Curious what's coming next? See the Roadmap.
