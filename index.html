About The Economic Atlas

The Economic Atlas removes the noise by being only data. No commentary, no forecasts, no opinion -- just official figures, rebuilt automatically from source every hour.

Every number traces back to one of: ONS, FRED, Eurostat, e-Stat and the Bank of Japan, MoSPI, the Bank of Korea, the OECD, and the World Bank. Compare, My Dashboard and the country pages are just different ways of looking at that same data, never a different version of it.
