How to read this page

  • Series is the series' own label: the measure, the publisher's identifier and, where the publisher states it, the seasonal adjustment. Select an indicator to see its chart and citation.
  • Adjustment is shown only when the label or the publisher's documented convention establishes it. Otherwise it says Not stated; we do not guess. Interest rates are Not applicable.
  • Not tracked means we hold no series for that core indicator. Nothing is estimated or filled in. Euro area members share the euro's own exchange rate, and the US dollar is the base currency, so neither is listed as missing for them.
  • The GDP growth ranking compares every economy for one quarter: the newest quarter that at least 75% of the economies with a usable growth series have published.
