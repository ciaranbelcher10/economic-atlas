     updated on schedule awaiting next release

    On the roadmap: federal debt stock, and a breakdown of where public money goes.

    Data: FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (IMF, OECD), the OECD and the World Bank. Series codes are shown with each metric; figures are the latest published observations and are subject to revision. Italy's trade balance, exports and imports are sourced from Eurostat directly in euros (unlike most other countries, where a combined figure is derived from a US-dollar-denominated OECD series), covering total trade including with other EU/eurozone members. Government debt and the deficit are also sourced from Eurostat. CPI's exact FRED series ID was extended from the Eurozone aggregate's own naming pattern rather than individually confirmed beforehand. The 10-year bond yield uses quarterly (not monthly) data, since search results for the monthly series were contradictory about whether it's still updating. Productivity (GDP per hour worked) is currently only tracked for the UK and US, not yet for Italy. Ideas or corrections: get in touch. Privacy · Terms.
