South Africa GDP in context

Worked out from the published series shown above.

In South Africa, GDP rose by R427bn over the year to Q2 2026, from R7.5tn in Q2 2025.

That is the highest reading in a record that starts in Q4 1993.

Over the past five years it has ranged from R6.0tn in Q2 2021 to R7.9tn in Q2 2026.
