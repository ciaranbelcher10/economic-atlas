India GDP in context

Worked out from the published series shown above.

In India, GDP rose by ₹29.7tn over the year to Q2 2026, from ₹324.0tn in Q2 2025.

That is the highest reading in a record that starts in Q1 2005.

Over the past five years it has ranged from ₹203.4tn in Q2 2021 to ₹353.6tn in Q2 2026.
