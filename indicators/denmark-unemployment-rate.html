Unemployment Rate

Source: FRED series LRHUTTTTDKM156S · OECD harmonized, monthly.

The share of people aged 15 and over who want a job and are actively looking, but can't find one. This measure reads higher than Denmark's commonly-cited national unemployment rate -- a methodology/definition difference between OECD-harmonized and national survey figures, not a data error.
