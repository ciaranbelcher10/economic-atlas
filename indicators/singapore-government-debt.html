Central government debt only, not the broader general-government measure used on most other country pages here -- no verified general-government series was found for Singapore during this build. Singapore's debt figures are also unusual because most government debt is issued to develop domestic capital markets and fund the reserves system, not to finance a deficit -- unlike most countries, this is not primarily a sign of fiscal strain.
