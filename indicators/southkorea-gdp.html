South Korea GDP in context

Worked out from the published series shown above.

In South Korea, GDP rose by ₩358.7tn over the year to Q2 2026, from ₩2,603.3tn in Q2 2025.

That is the highest reading in a record that starts in Q4 1970.

Over the past five years it has ranged from ₩2,133.8tn in Q2 2021 to ₩2,962.0tn in Q2 2026.
