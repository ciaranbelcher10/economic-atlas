Source: FRED series GGGDTATRA188N · IMF World Economic Outlook, general government, % of GDP, annual. The IMF publishes this as an outturn only up to the year shown; its figures for later years are projections, held in a separate series that this site does not publish. So the year under the figure is the most recent actual available, not a sign the feed has broken.

Turkey is neither an EU nor EEA/EFTA member, so the Eurostat quarterly datasets used for European pages don't cover it; these are IMF-WEO annual figures instead, which is why Turkey's fiscal charts update yearly rather than quarterly.
