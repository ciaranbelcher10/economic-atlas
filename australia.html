Known gap: CPI inflation isn't shown yet. Australia's FRED CPI mirrors are dead — the same systemic failure that hit Japan's, India's and Canada's CPI: FRED's OECD "MEI" vintage CPI family was discontinued en masse around March 2025. The ABS clearly still publishes CPI directly (quarterly, plus a monthly indicator since Oct 2022), but the live replacement route needs its own verification pass before going live here — shown instead is the 10-year government bond yield, which is genuinely live.
